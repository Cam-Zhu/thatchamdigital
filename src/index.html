Questions people ask

What's the catch at £350? +

I'm building up a portfolio of local work, so this is a founding rate for the first handful of Thatcham and Newbury businesses.

Do I actually own it? +

Yes. The domain goes in your name, the site files are yours, and if you ever want to take it somewhere else I'll hand the lot over without a fuss. Nothing is held hostage.

I haven't got any words or photos. +

Most people haven't. I'll tidy up whatever you've got as part of the £350. If you'd rather I wrote the whole thing from scratch, that would be extra. We can discuss and I'll tell you what it costs before I start.

Why not just use Wix or Squarespace? +

You can, and for some people that's the right answer. But it's an evening a week for a month, it keeps charging you, and the result usually looks like the template it came from. This way you get it done, done properly, and you get your evenings back.

What about Google Maps listings? +

Your Google Business Profile is often worth more than the website for getting the phone to ring, so it's worth doing. Google requires the owner to verify it in person with a video, which means it has to be you rather than me. I'll talk you through exactly what to film, free, whether or not you're a client.

How long does it really take? +

Two weeks from the day your content lands with me. The bit that slows projects down is almost always waiting on photos, so the sooner you send them the sooner you're live.
